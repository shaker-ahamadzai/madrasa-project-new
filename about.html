About Noor Islamic Academy

Our Mission

At Noor Islamic Academy, we are dedicated to nurturing young minds with the light of Islamic knowledge and modern education. Our mission is to develop well-rounded individuals who excel in both religious and academic pursuits while embodying Islamic values in their daily lives.

Our Core Values

Islamic Excellence

Committed to teaching authentic Islamic knowledge and values

Academic Excellence

Providing high-quality education in all subjects

Community Service

Fostering a spirit of giving and community involvement

Character Building

Developing strong moral character and leadership skills

Our History

Founded with a vision to create a center of excellence in Islamic education, Noor Islamic Academy has been serving the community since its establishment. We have grown from a small learning center to a comprehensive educational institution, touching the lives of countless students and families.
